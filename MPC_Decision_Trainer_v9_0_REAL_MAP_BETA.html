COP symbology note

Friendly and enemy unit markers use solid affiliation fills: blue for friendly and red for hostile, with white outlines and white function identifiers such as RECON, ANTI-TANK, MORTAR, ARMOR, C-UAS, and HQ. Suspected and last-known hostile contacts retain dashed/dotted white outlines to preserve certainty. Echelon indicators remain above the unit marker.
